Frequently Asked
Before You Call
What is the difference between the three series?
Ark Z1 lifts are high-capacity traction lifts for mid- to high-rise buildings. Ark Rise lifts are home lifts with no pit or headroom. Vision lifts are compact, self-supporting turnkey lifts for tight indoor spaces.
Which lifts run on single-phase power?
The Ark Rise series and the Vision series run on single-phase power. The Ark Z1 series needs three-phase power.
What does "Infinity" mean?
Infinity models add premium cabin interiors, lighting, flooring and personalised control panels to the standard model.
Rope or belt drive – which is better?
Both give smooth, efficient travel. We recommend the drive that best suits your building after the site survey.
Is every lift tested before handover?
Yes. Every installation is load- and function-tested on site before we hand it over.
How much space does a Vision lift need?
Vision 360 has a 1050 mm external diameter and Vision Cube a 1050 × 1050 mm footprint, with just a 150 mm pit.
Can Ark Rise be installed outdoors?
Yes. Ark Rise is designed for both indoor and outdoor installation, with no pit or headroom needed.
Can I get copies of the certificates?
Yes. Contact us and we will share the documents for your chosen model.
